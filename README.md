## I'm a runner, a musician, and a long distance enthusiast
- I run 6 days a week. I find that I'm always happiest and at my best when I'm moving my body consistently.
- I've run the [Vermont 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermont_100_Mile_Endurance_Run), the oldest 100 mile foot race on the East Coast. I've also ran numerous 50 mile races ([Philmont 50](https://runsignup.com/Race/NM/Cimarron/PhilmontTrailRace), [Heartland 50](https://ultrasignup.com/register.aspx?did=127331), etc), many more marathons (current PR of 3h03m19s at [Napa](https://napavalleymarathon.org/race-information/)), and raced [Unbound Gravel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unbound_Gravel) on my trusty steel frame 2 speed bike.
- I'm a Pianist and Trumpet player, I really enjoy Jazz, particularly hard bop, post bop, and bossa nova. Some of my favorite pianists include Bud Powell, Red Garland, Ryo Fukui, and Oscar Peterson.
- I was formerly in a Band, [House of Artifacts](https://houseofartifacts.bandcamp.com/album/house-of-artifacts). Checkout out our first, self titled, album on [Vinyl](https://gryphonswing.com/products/house-of-artifacts-house-of-artifacts-lp), [CD](https://gryphonswing.com/products/house-of-artifacts-house-of-artifacts-cd), [Casette](https://gryphonswing.com/products/house-of-artifacts-house-of-artifacts-cassette), [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/482dIOb2MbUQDStpGP2dms?si=pUoxAwTxST-FHUy5_GRBEQ), [Bandcamp](https://houseofartifacts.bandcamp.com/album/house-of-artifacts), [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/house-of-artifacts/1802005185), or anywhere else you can stream/purchase music! Our second album is releasing when it's done.
- I biked the 4,200 mile [TransAmerica Bike Trail](https://www.adventurecycling.org/routes-and-maps/adventure-cycling-route-network/transamerica-trail/) in 2022, thru hiked the entire 3,100 mile [Continental Divide Trail](https://continentaldividetrail.org/) in 2021, and thru hiked the whole 273 mile [Long Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Trail) of Vermont in 2020.
- I recieved my Masters in Computer Science from [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/) in May 2022, having received my Bachelors in Civil Engineering also from Northeastern, in May 2020.
